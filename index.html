Default Identity

Your name and company are used as defaults across the app — in email footers, AI-generated sign-offs, and as the fallback From name when outreach accounts don't specify one.

Legacy SMTP Fallback

These credentials are only used when no outreach accounts are configured. If you're using Brevo Relay or multiple SMTP accounts (configured below), you can leave these blank.

Outreach Accounts

These are your primary sending accounts used for all campaign emails and follow-ups. Add multiple accounts and the engine automatically rotates between them based on each account's daily limit. For Brevo Relay mode, all agents share a single SMTP connection to Brevo and send from your subdomain.

Configure Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) to send emails from your subdomain (e.g. agent@outreach.yourdomain.com). All Brevo-relay agents share this single SMTP key.

Cloudflare Email Routing

When using Brevo Relay with Cloudflare Email Routing, all replies to *@outreach.yourdomain.com are forwarded to a single Gmail inbox. Configure that inbox here — the reply scanner will use it instead of scanning each agent's inbox separately.
